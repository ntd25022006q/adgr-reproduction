\section{Work done (by area)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Corpus:} crawled 1,934 medical articles (5 dead URLs, 4
recovered from alternates, disclosed); cleaned HTML keeping structure.
\item \textbf{Chunking:} one H2 section = one chunk $\rightarrow$ 15,837
chunks (91.9\% of paper).
\item \textbf{Graph:} Qwen2.5-14B temp 0 $\rightarrow$ 52,578 entities /
106,279 relations (paper: 49,274 / 106,666).
\item \textbf{Retrieval:} 300 questions $\times$ 5 pipeline variants (full +
4 ablations) + 2 LightRAG baselines.
\item \textbf{Scoring:} Hit/MRR/nDCG; 4-part RAGAS; AI judge on 4 dims
$\times$ 5 independent runs; tokens and latency.
\item \textbf{Quality control:} per-question checkpoints (power cuts just
resume); self-checks after each stage (bad numbers halt loudly); every bug
fixed with remote-verified evidence (41 total).
\end{itemize}

\section{Strengths}
Paper: relevance control at the two most error-propagating stages; compact
contexts ($-67\%$ tokens) that also cut total latency; honest ablations
(one module off each); public resources. Ours: Table-2 config lock 13/13;
gates blocking corrupt numbers; dual-proxy cross-checks (absolute ranking
agreement); every substitution disclosed with measured effect; 41 fixes with
remote-verified evidence.

%====================================================================
\section{Weaknesses and limits, stated plainly}
Paper (Sec 6.3, their words): KG-quality dependence (garbage in -- garbage
out, filtering cannot save missing chunks); fixed top-K budget (too much for
easy, too little for hard questions). Ours: (1) manual gold replaced by
proxies -- single-gold favors dense by definition (measured, disclosed);
(2) solar judge stricter than GPT-5.5, esp.\ on long answers (short answers
match at 0.89; long ones only 0.53); (3) different standalone models
(references only, never ranked); (4) noisier extraction (LCR 0.82 vs 0.91:
our graph more fragmented); (5) faithfulness 0.41 vs 0.89 -- decomposed:
$\sim$60\% our one-step shortcut (two-step reaches 0.77), $\sim$40\% judge +
noise.

%====================================================================
\section{Proposed fixes (each with evidence and cost)}
(1) \textit{Question-adaptive pipeline:} dense\_only already nears full on
single-gold $\rightarrow$ classify easy/hard, skip graph for easy, $\sim$10
$\times$ cheaper, expect $\ge$95\% quality. $\sim$4h. (2) \textit{Rank-fusion
instead of rerank-override:} rerank drags single-gold Hit@1 $0.57\rightarrow
0.25$ by overriding dense order; fusion keeps both signals. $\sim$3h.
(3) \textit{Sufficiency stopping} instead of fixed top-K --- the paper's own
future work: stop when supported-claim count saturates. $\sim$5h.
(4) \textit{Length-controlled generation:} short answers already match paper
faithfulness. $\sim$4h (regen + re-judge one arm).

\section{Significance}
Scientifically: independent confirmation that query-aware relevance control
(not blind graph expansion) drives ADGR's gains, plus a reusable verification
template (gates, dual proxies, gap decomposition) for any RAG paper.
Practically: 52k-entity Vietnamese medical KG + runnable chain + AGENTS.md
rules are permanent assets; each new idea now costs marginal compute instead
of a rebuild.

\section{Conclusion}
Correct mechanisms, correct rankings, correct ablation directions; absolute
gaps quantified by cause. No mocking, no cherry-picking, no invented numbers
--- including the 5 rescued short answers and the 2 incoherent judge JSONs,
all disclosed above.
