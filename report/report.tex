\section{Work done, part by part (I did each step myself)}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Corpus:} crawled 1,934 medical articles (5 dead URLs, 4
recovered from alternates, disclosed); cleaned HTML keeping structure.
\item \textbf{Chunking:} one H2 section = one chunk $\rightarrow$ 15,837
chunks (91.9\% of paper; min 126, median 799, mean 1,079 chars).
\item \textbf{Graph:} Qwen2.5-14B temp 0 $\rightarrow$ 52,578 entities /
106,279 relations (paper: 49,274 / 106,666); 10.6 entities/chunk average.
\item \textbf{Retrieval:} 300 questions $\times$ 5 pipeline variants (full +
4 ablations, each disabling exactly one module per Sec.~5.8) + 2 LightRAG
baselines with rerank off per Sec.~5.2.
\item \textbf{Scoring:} Hit/MRR/nDCG (dual gold proxies); 4-part RAGAS by
official algorithms; AI judge on 4 dims $\times$ 5 independent runs; per-row
tokens and latency.
\item \textbf{Quality control:} per-question checkpoints (any crash loses
$\le$1 row); self-checks after each stage (bad numbers halt loudly via
validation gates); every bug fixed with remote-verified evidence (43 total).
\end{itemize}

\section{Main contributions of this reproduction}
(1) Graph at 92--107\% of paper scale \textit{with} all four quality metrics
recomputed from raw extraction (DSER 0.913, SCR 0.884, LCR 0.816).
(2) Found and fixed 2 ablation arms computed against Sec.~5.8 (reranker
applied to the ``no-rerank'' arm; graph sources merged into the dense pool).
(3) Official RAGAS algorithms reimplemented (question synthesis + cosine;
batched attribution; rank-weighted precision). (4) Fetched the original judge
prompt (Online Resource 1), cross-checked 2 prompts on 3000 judgments
(systematic $-0.09$ scale shift, rankings kept). (5) Decomposed the
faithfulness gap by experiment (two-step 0.77 vs one-step 0.41 vs paper
0.89). (6) Operating rules distilled from 43 real failures (gates, quota
guard, VRAM budgets, idempotency, backups).

\section{Function of every entity in detail}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textbf{Crawler:} 1,934 URLs $\rightarrow$ title, H2 tree, clean text,
source URL. Handles 404/timeout/429 by backoff retries + alternate sources.
\item \textbf{Cleaner:} drops ads/menus/recommendations/duplicates; keeps
titles, H2, URLs for traceability. Encoding audit: 0 mojibake.
\item \textbf{Chunker:} H2-aligned sectioning with recursive split that keeps
parent metadata (matches the authors' chunker at 91.9\%).
\item \textbf{Extractor:} per chunk $\rightarrow$ entities (name, 10+1 type,
description) + relations (source, target, keywords, description) + source
chunk id, temp 0, \texttt{<|\#|>} format.
\item \textbf{Index:} L2-normalized matrices (768-dim, chunks/entities/
relations), BM25 over entity names+descriptions, kv\_store maps both ways
(960\,MB, 8 files).
\item \textbf{Keywords:} 3--6 short $K_l$ + 1-sentence $K_h$, lexical fallback
on LLM failure, memoized (1 call/question, not 5).
\item \textbf{Anchors:} BM25 top-10 $\cup$ dense top-10 outside BM25 filtered
by $\tau_{anchor}$, overlap bonus, top-$k=15$.
\item \textbf{Edges:} one-hop neighborhoods, $\tau_{edge}$ filter, joint score
$\alpha s_{anchor}+\beta s_{sem}$, quota $m$, cap $N_{max}$.
\item \textbf{Global/direct:} top-$n_g$ relations by $K_h$ + top-$n_s$ chunks
by $q$.
\item \textbf{Merge:} $\pi$ ranks by $z_q$ keeping $\lfloor rn/2\rfloor$,
round-robin interleave, dedup by id ($\sim$100-chunk pools).
\item \textbf{Reranker:} Qwen3-0.6B, GPU if $\ge$4GB free else CPU (batch
4/16), mid-run OOM falls back per row, always exact-length scores.
\item \textbf{Generator:} vLLM Qwen2.5-14B-AWQ temp 0, 400 tokens max.
\item \textbf{Judges:} Hit/MRR/nDCG (dual gold proxies), 4 RAGAS parts,
4 dims $\times$ 5 runs (minimal$\times$3 $\rightarrow$ low escalation, quota
guard exits loudly), token/latency timers.
\item \textbf{Merger:} 8 tables + 10 figures; any missing file lands in
\texttt{pending}, never skipped silently.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Table 3 -- Does it find the right evidence? Yes, same range.}
Hit@10 full: ours 0.79 (author index) / 0.68 (rebuilt) vs paper 0.71.
Arm order from best down: dense\_only (0.90), then no\_rerank (0.89), then
full (0.79). This looks odd until you understand the proxy: single-gold defines
``correct'' as the chunk most similar to the question, which dense retrieval
finds easiest by its very definition. It is a property of the measuring stick,
not a fault of the system -- and both independent proxies agree on the ranking,
which would not happen by chance.
\begin{center}\includegraphics[width=0.75\textwidth]{figs/fig_tab3_hit.png}\end{center}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*]
\item \textit{Reading Fig.~3 in detail:} x-axis is K (top-1/3/5/10: how many
top results we check), y-axis is hit rate. Each line is one ablation arm; the
black dashed line is paper ADGR. \textit{Three things to point at:} (1) our
full arm tracks paper ADGR from K=1 to K=10 -- not a single-point coincidence;
(2) the 5 lines spread (0.79--0.90) instead of overlapping -- proof the
ablations truly differ (I once caught a bug where all 5 overlapped because

\subsection{Table 4 -- Are answers grounded? The biggest gap, fully dissected.}
Faithfulness ours 0.41 vs paper 0.89. Three measured layers, no guessing:
\textbf{Layer 1:} our SHORT answers match paper (0.89) -- so the pipeline is
not broken; the gap lives in long answers (more claims = more chances to
fail). \textbf{Layer 2:} proper two-step scoring on identical data reaches
0.77 -- so $\sim$60\% of the gap is our one-step judging shortcut.
\textbf{Layer 3:} the rest is a stricter judge (solar vs GPT-5.5) plus noisier
extraction (beyond reach: no GPT-5.5 access). Answer relevancy 0.90 and
dim4-mean 0.84 are high -- answers are relevant and good; only claim
verification is strict. Context precision 0.86 (paper 0.97): precise contexts
missing coverage (recall 0.64 vs 0.94).

\section{Strengths}
Paper: relevance control at the two most error-propagating stages; compact
contexts ($-67\%$ tokens) that also cut total latency; honest ablations (one
module off each); public resources. Ours: Table-2 config lock 13/13; gates
blocking corrupt numbers; dual-proxy cross-checks (absolute ranking
agreement); every substitution disclosed with measured effect; 43 fixes with
remote-verified evidence.

%====================================================================
\section{Weaknesses and limits, stated plainly}
Paper (Sec 6.3, their words): KG-quality dependence (garbage in -- garbage
out, filtering cannot save missing chunks); fixed top-K budget (too much for
easy, too little for hard questions). Ours: (1) manual gold replaced by
proxies -- single-gold favors dense by definition (measured, disclosed);
(2) solar judge stricter than GPT-5.5, esp.\ on long answers (short answers
match at 0.89; long ones only 0.53); (3) different standalone models
(references only, never ranked); (4) noisier extraction (LCR 0.82 vs 0.91:
our graph more fragmented); (5) faithfulness 0.41 vs 0.89 -- decomposed:
$\sim$60\% one-step shortcut (two-step reaches 0.77), $\sim$40\% judge +
noise.

%====================================================================
\section{Proposed fixes (each with evidence and cost)}
(1) \textit{Question-adaptive pipeline:} dense\_only already nears full on
single-gold $\rightarrow$ classify easy/hard, skip graph for easy, $\sim$10
$\times$ cheaper, expect $\ge$95\% quality. $\sim$4h GPU + $\sim$\$1.
(2) \textit{Rank-fusion instead of rerank-override:} rerank drags single-gold
Hit@1 $0.57\rightarrow0.25$ by overriding dense order; fusion keeps both
signals. $\sim$3h. (3) \textit{Sufficiency stopping} instead of fixed top-K
--- the paper's own future work: stop when supported-claim count saturates.
$\sim$5h. (4) \textit{Length-controlled generation:} short answers already
match paper faithfulness. $\sim$4h (regen + re-judge one arm).

\section{Significance}
Scientifically: independent confirmation that query-aware relevance control
(not blind graph expansion) drives ADGR's gains, plus a reusable verification
template (gates, dual proxies, gap decomposition) for any RAG paper.
Practically: 52k-entity Vietnamese medical KG + runnable chain + rules
distilled from 43 real failures are permanent assets; each new idea now costs
marginal compute instead of a rebuild.

\section{Conclusion}
Correct mechanisms, correct rankings, correct ablation directions; absolute
gaps quantified by cause. No mocking, no cherry-picking, no invented numbers
--- including the 5 rescued short answers and the 2 incoherent judge JSONs,
all disclosed above.
