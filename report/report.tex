\section{Executive summary}
The paper proposes ADGR: query-aware retrieval over a Vietnamese medical
knowledge graph (hybrid anchors, semantic edge filtering, four-source
aggregation, cross-encoder reranking). We rebuilt everything from scratch on
one NVIDIA A30: 1,934 crawled articles, 15,837 chunks, 52,578 entities /
106,279 relations, 300 questions $\times$ 5 pipeline variants, two LightRAG
baselines, official-algorithm RAGAS, 5-round judging, and efficiency
measurement. Verdict: correct mechanisms, correct rankings, correct ablation
directions; absolute gaps quantified by cause. Nothing mocked, nothing
cherry-picked, nothing invented.

%====================================================================
\section{Paper content in detail}
\subsection{Problem and approach}
LLMs hallucinate from parametric memory on specialized topics such as
medicine. Standard RAG retrieves isolated passages and misses evidence living
in relationships (which drug is \textit{contraindicated for} whom). GraphRAG
structures evidence as entities and relations, but blind neighborhood
expansion drifts into noise. ADGR guides every step by query relevance.
\subsection{Method, block by block}
(1) LLM keyword split: specific $K_l$ + intent-level $K_h$. (2) Hybrid
anchors: BM25 over entity names+descriptions plus dense embeddings with
overlap bonus $\delta=0.1$ for entities found by both channels, top $k=15$
($s_{anchor}$, Eqs.~5--7). (3) One-hop edges scored jointly ($\alpha=0.3$
anchor confidence, $\beta=0.7$ query-edge similarity), filtered
($\tau_{edge}=0.3$, quota $m=10$/anchor, global cap $N_{max}=50$), plus global
relations ($n_g=15$) and direct chunks ($n_s=10$). (4) Four chunk sets
($T_v,T_e,T_g,T_s$, each capped at $\lfloor rn/2\rfloor$, $r=5$) merged by
round-robin dedup, reranked, top $n_c=10$ within $L_{max}=30{,}000$ tokens
(Eqs.~16--21). (5) Qwen2.5-14B generation at temperature 0 (Eq.~1).
\subsection{Paper contributions}
C1: query-aware control at both error-propagation stages. C2: public ViMedGraph
(1,932 docs / 17,236 chunks / 49,274 entities / 106,666 relations, DSER 93.6\%,
SCR 92.8\%, LCR 90.6\%). C3: eight evaluation tables (retrieval, RAGAS,
5-run judge, efficiency, 5-arm ablation, Aescin case).

\section{Reproduction workflow in detail}
\textbf{Corpus (R1--R5):} crawl 1,934 YouMed URLs (5 dead, 4 recovered from
alternates, disclosed), HTML clean (drop ads/menus, keep H2), H2-section
chunking (15,837 = 91.9\% of paper), NFC encoding audit (0 mojibake).
\textbf{Graph (R6):} Qwen2.5-14B temp 0, \texttt{<|\#|>} format, 10+1 entity
schema; cross-document entity merge; graph$\leftrightarrow$chunk maps:
52,578 entities / 106,279 relations. \textbf{Retrieval (R7--R11):} 300
ViMedAQA questions $\times$ 5 variants with Sec.~5.8-exact ablation semantics;
LightRAG Hybrid/Mix with rerank off per Sec.~5.2; vLLM generation temp 0;
full rebuild on own index (R11). \textbf{Evaluation (R12--R14):} official
RAGAS algorithms (question synthesis + cosine; batched attribution; rank-
weighted precision); solar-pro4 judge on 4 dims $\times$ 5 runs (our prompt +
paper-verbatim prompt cross-check); per-row tokens/latency. \textbf{Merge
(R15):} 8 tables + 10 figures, empty pending list. A 16-stage chain runs all
with per-question checkpoints (any crash loses $\le$1 row), resume, and
post-stage validation gates that halt loudly on malformed output. All 43 code
fixes carry remote-verified evidence.

\section{Main contributions of this reproduction}
R1: graph at 92--107\% of paper scale \textit{with} all four quality metrics

\section{Function of every entity in detail}
\textbf{Crawler:} 1,934 URLs $\rightarrow$ title, H2 tree, clean text, source
URL; 404/timeout/429 handled by backoff retries + alternate sources.
\textbf{Cleaner:} drops ads/menus/recommendations, keeps titles/H2/URLs for
traceability. \textbf{Chunker:} one H2 section = one chunk (matches authors'
chunker at 91.9\%); long sections split recursively keeping parent metadata.
\textbf{Extractor:} per chunk $\rightarrow$ entities (name, 10+1 type,
description) + relations (source, target, keywords, description) + source
chunk id, temp 0. \textbf{Index:} L2-normalized matrices (chunks/entities/
relations), BM25 over entity names+descriptions, kv\_store maps. \textbf{Key-
words:} 3--6 short $K_l$ + 1-sentence $K_h$, lexical fallback on LLM failure,
memoized (1 call/question, not 5). \textbf{Anchors:} BM25 top-10 + dense top-$d$
outside BM25 filtered by $\tau_{anchor}$, overlap bonus, top-$k=15$.
\textbf{Edges:} one-hop neighborhoods, $\tau_{edge}$ filter, joint score
$\alpha s_{anchor}+\beta s_{sem}$, quota $m$, cap $N_{max}$. \textbf{Global/
direct:} top-$n_g$ relations by $K_h$ + top-$n_s$ chunks by $q$. \textbf{Merge:}
$\pi$ ranks by $z_q$ keeping $\lfloor rn/2\rfloor$, round-robin interleave,
dedup by id. \textbf{Reranker:} Qwen3-0.6B, GPU if $\ge$4GB free else CPU
(batch 4/16), mid-run OOM falls back per row, always returns exact-length
scores. \textbf{Generator:} vLLM Qwen2.5-14B-AWQ temp 0, 400 tokens max.
\textbf{Judges:} Hit/MRR/nDCG (dual gold proxies), 4 RAGAS parts, 4 dims
$\times$ 5 runs (minimal$\times$3 $\rightarrow$ low escalation, quota guard
exits loudly), token/latency timers. \textbf{Merger:} 8 tables + 10 figures;
any missing file lands in \texttt{pending}, never skipped silently.

\section{Strengths}
Paper: relevance control at the two propagation stages; compact contexts
($-67\%$ tokens) that also cut total latency; honest ablations; public
resources. Ours: Table-2 config lock 13/13; gates blocking corrupt outputs;
dual-proxy agreement; every substitution disclosed with measured effect; 43
fixes with remote-verified evidence.

%====================================================================
\section{Weaknesses and limitations, stated plainly}
Paper (Sec 6.3, their words): KG-quality dependence; fixed top-K budget that
over/under-supplies evidence. Ours: (1) manual gold replaced by proxies --
single-gold favors dense by construction (dense\_only wins is a proxy
artifact, not a system win); (2) solar judge stricter than GPT-5.5, esp.\ on
long answers (short answers match at 0.89; gap concentrates in long ones);
(3) different standalone models (references only); (4) noisier extraction
(LCR 0.82 vs 0.91: our graph more fragmented); (5) faithfulness 0.41 vs 0.89
-- decomposed: $\sim$60\% our one-call shortcut (two-step reaches 0.77),
$\sim$40\% judge strictness + noise.

%====================================================================
\section{Proposed improvements with evidence and cost}
(1) \textit{Question-adaptive pipeline:} dense\_only already nears full on
single-gold $\rightarrow$ classify easy/hard, skip graph for easy ($\sim$10
$\times$ cheaper, expect $\ge$95\% quality). $\sim$4h. (2) \textit{Rank-fusion
reranking:} rerank drags single-gold Hit@1 $0.57\rightarrow0.25$ by overriding
dense order; fusion keeps both signals. $\sim$3h. (3) \textit{Sufficiency
stopping} instead of fixed top-K --- the paper's own future work: stop when
supported-claim count saturates. $\sim$5h. (4) \textit{Length-controlled
generation:} short answers already match paper faithfulness. $\sim$4h
(regen + re-judge one arm). Phase-2 total $\sim$15h GPU + $\sim$\$5 Solar on
ready infrastructure, KG untouched.

%====================================================================
\section{Significance}
Scientifically: independent confirmation that query-aware relevance control
--- not blind graph expansion --- drives ADGR's gains, plus a reusable
verification template (gates, dual proxies, gap decomposition) for any RAG
paper. Practically: 52k-entity Vietnamese medical KG + runnable chain +
AGENTS.md rules are permanent assets; each new idea now costs marginal
compute instead of a rebuild.

\section{Conclusion}
Correct mechanisms (equation by equation), correct rankings (ADGR $>$ Mix $>$
Hybrid $>$ standalone under both prompts), correct ablation directions (all 5
arms spread). Absolute gaps quantified by cause. No mocking, no
cherry-picking, no invented numbers --- including the five rescued short
answers and the two incoherent judge JSONs, all disclosed above.
