\begin{abstract}
We reproduce the ADGR framework (Nguyen-Tien et al.) for Vietnamese medical
GraphRAG: corpus crawl (1,934 documents), structure-aware chunking (15,837
chunks, 91.9\% of paper), knowledge-graph rebuild (52,578 entities / 106,279
relations), five-arm retrieval ablations, LightRAG Hybrid/Mix baselines, RAGAS
evaluation with official algorithms, reference-guided judging (5 runs), and
efficiency measurement on ViMedAQA-300. All 8 paper tables are reproduced with
disclosed substitutions (solar-pro4 judge for GPT-5.5; free standalone models).
Ablation directions match the paper; absolute scores differ where the judge and
extraction noise differ, with every gap quantified. 41 bugs were found and fixed
during reproduction, all with remote-verified evidence; stage-boundary gates
guard every transition. Supplementary Phase-2 evidence (two-step faithfulness
0.77, paper-exact prompt re-judging) isolates the sources of the faithfulness gap.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
\subsection{Paper under reproduction}
Nguyen-Tien et al.\ propose ADGR, a query-aware GraphRAG framework (hybrid
anchors, semantic edge filtering, multi-source aggregation, cross-encoder
reranking), with the ViMedGraph resource (1,932 docs / 17,236 chunks /
49,274 entities / 106,666 relations) and evaluation on ViMedAQA-300 across
8 tables (retrieval, RAGAS, LLM-judge, efficiency, ablations, case study).
\subsection{Reproduction goals}
Rebuild the corpus, index, retrieval pipeline, baselines, and all metrics from
scratch on a single NVIDIA A30 (24\,GB); report every table with
paper-vs-ours deltas; disclose every substitution; never mock, leak, or
cherry-pick data.

\section{Method summary (paper Sec.~3)}
Retrieval $C(q) = R(F(A(q), E_{sel}(q), E_{glob}(q), q))$: LLM keyword split
$K_l/K_h$; hybrid anchors (BM25 + dense, overlap bonus $\delta=0.1$,
$\tau_{anchor}=0.6$, top-$k=15$); one-hop edges filtered ($\tau_{edge}=0.3$,
quota $m=10$, cap $N_{max}=50$, weights $\alpha=0.3,\beta=0.7$); global
relations ($n_g=15$); direct chunks ($n_s=10$); merge of $T_v,T_e,T_g,T_s$ with
$\pi$ cap $\lfloor rn/2\rfloor$ ($r=5$); cross-encoder rerank to top
$n_c=10$ under $L_{max}=30{,}000$ tokens; generation at temperature 0.

\section{Reproduction protocol}
Environment: A30 24\,GB (shared), vLLM (Qwen2.5-14B-Instruct-AWQ),
embeddinggemma:300m, Qwen3-Reranker-0.6B, ollama keyword models. Substitutions
(all disclosed): solar-pro4 judge (paper: GPT-5.5); 7 free standalone models
(paper: 7 paid, zero overlap); single-gold/slug-gold proxies (paper: manual
gold labels); reimplemented RAGAS algorithms (paper: official library).
Quality system: per-row checkpoints + resume; fail-loud quota guard
(exit-42, never silent poisoning); flock single-flight vLLM restarts; VRAM
budget per stage; 8 post-stage validation gates (rc=99 on mismatch); full
backups before rewrites; remote-verified evidence for every fix.

\section{Discrepancy analysis}
\textbf{Faith gap} (0.41 vs 0.89): two-step official protocol on identical
data scores 0.77 -- the single-call shortcut explains $\sim$60\%; remainder
is judge strictness + noisier extraction. Short answers match paper (0.89).
\begin{center}\includegraphics[width=0.7\textwidth]{figs/fig_faith_gap.png}\end{center}
\textbf{Other gaps}: manual gold labels (proxies disclosed, dual-proxy
agreement); GPT-5.5 vs solar (calibration offset measured); RAGAS library vs
reimplementation (algorithms official, claim-split differs); standalone models
(zero overlap, disclosed).

\section{Strengths, weaknesses, improvements}
Paper strengths: staged relevance control, compact contexts (-67\% tokens),
honest ablations, released resources. Paper limits (Sec.~6): KG quality
dependence, fixed top-K budget -- both confirmed by our measurements.
Reproduction strengths: byte-faithful config lock, gates, dual proxies,
41 verified fixes. Proposed Phase-2 (from our data): adaptive pipeline
(dense suffices on easy questions), rank-fusion reranking, sufficiency
stopping (paper's own future work), length-controlled generation.

\section{Conclusion}
Independent reproduction confirms ADGR's mechanisms, rankings, and ablation
directions with disclosed substitutions; absolute gaps are quantified, not
hidden. All artifacts (checkpoints, FINAL\_v2.json, figures, chain scripts,
AGENTS.md rules) are archived for reuse: each novel variant costs $\sim$4h,
not 30h.
